# CRO/X second alpha — Archetype Map (proposal, updated 2026-11-15 with talent abilities)

Status: **proposal for review — nothing here is approved or built yet.**

The second alpha is a full rework. Instead of settings and lines per ability, everything branches from a small set of **archetypes**: kinds of combat action that every class shares. Each ability belongs to one archetype. How often something speaks, and whether it's a say, a yell or an emote, will be decided per archetype, and stock phrases get written per archetype (step 2).

Why: in play, too many lines and emotes made the addon feel fake. Lines should be rare enough to feel real. Earlier principles still guide this, but none of the first alpha's rules are binding.

**Coverage:** trainer abilities 1–60, WoW: Forever's new abilities, and every talent-tree ability that is a button you press. Talent abilities are marked with an asterisk (*). Passive talents aren't listed, since nothing is pressed.

## The tree

Six branches, 30 archetypes. "How often" is how often the button gets pressed in a normal fight, which is what should drive how rarely it speaks.

### Hurt — Dealing damage

| Archetype | What it is | How often it's pressed |
|---|---|---|
| **Opener** | The first blow — closing in or starting the fight | once a fight |
| **Filler** | Bread and butter — the button pressed most | constant |
| **Heavy hitter** | A big single hit on a cooldown or a long cast | often |
| **Area** | Hits everything nearby | often |
| **Upkeep** | DoTs, bleeds, stings, armor shred — kept rolling | constant |
| **Reactive** | Only usable after a dodge, block, parry or proc | often |
| **Finisher** | Execute range or a combo spender — ending it | once a fight |

### Hinder — Stopping the enemy

| Archetype | What it is | How often it's pressed |
|---|---|---|
| **Interrupt** | Cuts off a cast | situational |
| **Lockdown** | Takes them out of the fight — stun, fear, sheep, sleep, sap | once a fight |
| **Snare** | Slows, roots or pins | situational |
| **Weaken** | Curses and shouts that make the enemy hit softer | once a fight |

### Protect — Staying alive and holding the line

| Archetype | What it is | How often it's pressed |
|---|---|---|
| **Taunt** | Pulls the enemy onto you | situational |
| **Guard** | A short or small defense — block, shield, dodge | often |
| **Last stand** | The big defensive cooldown when it's going wrong | rare |
| **Escape** | Getting away — blink, sprint, vanish, play dead | situational |
| **Sneak** | Stealth and dirty tricks | situational |

### Mend — Healing and fixing

| Archetype | What it is | How often it's pressed |
|---|---|---|
| **Heal** | A single-target heal | constant |
| **Ward and renew** | Heal-over-time or a shield on an ally | often |
| **Group heal** | Heals the whole group | often |
| **Clutch save** | The emergency button — saves a life | rare |
| **Cleanse** | Removes a poison, disease, curse or magic | situational |
| **Raise** | Brings someone back mid-fight | rare |

### Empower — Making yourself or the group stronger

| Archetype | What it is | How often it's pressed |
|---|---|---|
| **Rally** | A group buff in the fight — shouts, auras, totems | once a fight |
| **Power-up** | The big offensive cooldown | rare |
| **Self-buff** | Armor, shields, seals, aspects — your own upkeep | out of combat |
| **Stance or form** | Switching stance, form or aura | situational |
| **Recharge** | Getting rage, mana or energy back | situational |
| **Bless an ally** | Long buffs on others, mostly out of combat | out of combat |

### Command — Pets, demons and minions

| Archetype | What it is | How often it's pressed |
|---|---|---|
| **Summon** | Calling a pet, demon or minion | rare |
| **Pet order** | Healing, boosting or commanding the pet | situational |

## The core map — every archetype across the classes

A class that isn't listed under an archetype has nothing of that kind. * = talent ability.

### Hurt

**Opener** — The first blow — closing in or starting the fight

- Warrior: Charge, Intercept
- Rogue: Ambush, Cheap Shot, Garrote
- Hunter: Hunter's Mark
- Mage: Pyroblast*
- Warlock: Immolate
- Priest: Holy Fire
- Paladin: Judgement
- Shaman: Lightning Bolt (first cast)
- Druid: Pounce, Ravage, Faerie Fire, Feral Charge*

**Filler** — Bread and butter — the button pressed most

- Warrior: Heroic Strike, Shoot / Throw, Slam
- Rogue: Sinister Strike, Backstab, Shank, Hemorrhage*, Throw / Shoot
- Hunter: Auto Shot, Arcane Shot, Raptor Strike
- Mage: Fireball, Frostbolt, Arcane Missiles, Scorch, Frostfire Bolt, Wand Shoot
- Warlock: Shadow Bolt, Searing Pain, Incinerate*, Wand Shoot
- Priest: Smite, Mind Flay*, Wand Shoot
- Paladin: Holy Strike
- Shaman: Lightning Bolt
- Druid: Wrath, Starfire, Claw, Shred, Maul

**Heavy hitter** — A big single hit on a cooldown or a long cast

- Warrior: Mortal Strike*, Bloodthirst*, Shield Slam*, Spearing Strike*
- Rogue: Mutilate*, Ghostly Strike*
- Hunter: Aimed Shot, Sniper Shot*
- Mage: Fire Blast, Arcane Blast*, Ice Lance*, Arcane Wrath*
- Warlock: Soul Fire, Conflagrate*, Death Coil
- Priest: Mind Blast, Penance (enemy)*, Starshards, Chastise
- Paladin: Exorcism, Holy Shock (enemy)*, Swift Judgement*
- Shaman: Lava Burst*, Stormstrike*
- Druid: Primal Bite*

**Area** — Hits everything nearby

- Warrior: Whirlwind, Cleave, Thunder Clap
- Rogue: Blade Flurry*
- Hunter: Multi-Shot, Volley, Explosive / Immolation Trap
- Mage: Arcane Explosion, Blizzard, Flamestrike, Blast Wave*, Cone of Cold, Frost Blast
- Warlock: Rain of Fire, Hellfire, Shadowfury*
- Priest: Holy Nova*
- Paladin: Consecration, Holy Wrath
- Shaman: Chain Lightning, Fire Nova / Magma Totem
- Druid: Swipe, Hurricane

**Upkeep** — DoTs, bleeds, stings, armor shred — kept rolling

- Warrior: Rend, Sunder Armor
- Rogue: Rupture, Expose Armor, Slice and Dice
- Hunter: Serpent Sting, Scorpid Sting, Viper Sting
- Warlock: Corruption, Bane of Agony / Doom / Havoc, Siphon Life*, Drain Life, Drain Mana, Wrack*
- Priest: Shadow Word: Pain, Devouring Plague, Vampiric Embrace*, Mana Burn
- Shaman: Flame Shock, Searing Totem
- Druid: Moonfire, Insect Swarm*, Rake, Lacerate*

**Reactive** — Only usable after a dodge, block, parry or proc

- Warrior: Overpower, Revenge
- Rogue: Riposte*
- Hunter: Mongoose Bite, Counterattack*

**Finisher** — Execute range or a combo spender — ending it

- Warrior: Execute, Victory Rush
- Rogue: Eviscerate, Venom*, Kidney Shot, Death Mark*
- Warlock: Shadowburn*, Drain Soul
- Priest: Shadow Word: Death
- Paladin: Hammer of Wrath
- Druid: Ferocious Bite, Rip

### Mend

**Heal** — A single-target heal

- Priest: Lesser Heal / Heal / Flash Heal / Greater Heal, Penance (friend)*
- Paladin: Holy Light / Flash of Light, Holy Shock (friend)*
- Shaman: Healing Wave / Lesser Healing Wave

- Priest: Renew, Power Word: Shield, Prayer of Mending*, Divine Grace
- Paladin: Oath of Light*, Light's Vigil*

**Group heal** — Heals the whole group

- Priest: Prayer of Healing, Lightwell*

**Raise** — Brings someone back mid-fight

- Shaman: Reincarnation
- Druid: Rebirth

**Rally** — A group buff in the fight — shouts, auras, totems

- Paladin: Seal of Righteousness / Command / Crusader / Fury, Seal of Light / Wisdom / Justice

- Warrior: Bloodrage, Berserker Rage, Reckless Swings*

- Warlock: Life Tap
- Shaman: Mana Spring / Mana Tide Totem
- Druid: Shifting Power*, Enrage, Innervate

**Bless an ally** — Long buffs on others, mostly out of combat

- Mage: Arcane Intellect / Brilliance, Amplify / Dampen Magic
- Warlock: Unending Breath / Detect Invisibility
- Priest: Power Word: Fortitude / Divine Spirit / Shadow Protection (and Prayers)
- Paladin: Blessings of Might / Wisdom / Kings, Salvation (and Greater)
- Druid: Thorns, Mark / Gift of the Wild

### Command

**Summon** — Calling a pet, demon or minion

- Hunter: Call Pet, Revive Pet
- Warlock: Summon Imp / Voidwalker / Succubus / Felhunter, Fel Domination*, Inferno, Enslave Demon

**Pet order** — Healing, boosting or commanding the pet

- Hunter: Summon Hawk*, Mend Pet, Dismiss Pet
- Warlock: Health Funnel, Demonic Sacrifice*, Soul Link*

## Quiet by default

Auto-attack, Auto Shot, wand Shoot, conjuring, portals and teleports, weapon imbues and poisons, tracking, mounts, Healthstone / Soulstone creation, rituals, out-of-combat resurrections, dismissing a pet.

## Judgement calls to check

- **One archetype per ability.** Abilities that do two things sit where their main use is (Thunder Clap is Area, not Snare; Cheap Shot is an Opener, not a Lockdown; Kidney Shot is a Finisher; Feral Charge is an Opener though it also interrupts).
- **Two abilities change by target:** Holy Shock and Penance are a Heavy hitter on an enemy and a Heal on a friend.
- **Totems and traps sit by what they do** (Earthbind Totem is a Snare, Freezing Trap is a Lockdown), not in a branch of their own.
- **Some classes have no true Opener** (shaman, paladin, warlock, priest, hunter). The map lists the usual first press, but the cleaner rule may be "the first attack of a fight counts as the Opener, whatever it is."
- **Paladin auras** are listed under Stance or form (they speak on a switch) though they also act as a Rally.
- **Seals** are listed as a Self-buff; they're pressed far more often than other self-buffs.
- **Evasion** is listed as a Last stand, Feint as a Guard.
- **Summon Hawk** is a Pet order, not a Summon: it's pressed as often as Arcane Shot.
- **Shifting Power** is a Recharge (it swaps energy and mana), not a Power-up.
- **Oath of Light and Light's Vigil** are under Ward and renew: both are placed on one ally during a fight.

## Not confirmed

The sources disagree or give no detail on these. Worth checking in game.

- **Venom (rogue):** a finisher in one source, a passive in another.
- **Lava Burst (shaman):** one source says a passive, Call of Thunder, replaced it on November 2.
- **Lightwell (priest):** a trainer ability in one source, cut in another.
- **Voice of Truth (paladin):** in Blizzard's deep dive as a new ability (immunity to silence and interrupts), but listed as removed later. Left out for now; it would be a Guard.
- **Turn Evil (paladin)** and **Demonic Brand (warlock):** listed as new with no description.
- **Shadowfury (warlock):** carried over from the first alpha; not found in the Forever talent lists.
- **Skyborne racial:** not known yet.
- Warrior abilities not in the first alpha's lists (Heroic Strike, Cleave, Sunder Armor, Slam, Disarm, Mocking Blow) are included from Classic; Forever may have changed some.
